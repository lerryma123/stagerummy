Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the privacy practices of Stage Rummy: Duck Card Game. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data processed through our mobile application.

1. Data Collection and Usage

As an independent developer, we do not directly collect, harvest, or store any personal data. However, the game utilizes standard mobile operating system services and third-party advertising tools that may process the following data types for functional purposes:

  • Identifiers: Mobile device identifiers (such as IDFA or Ad ID) and User IDs may be processed to deliver non-intrusive third-party advertisements and ensure proper app functionality.
  • Diagnostics: Technical data, including crash data and diagnostic reports, may be processed to help diagnose technical issues, fix bugs, and optimize overall game performance.

2. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain external servers to store personal user histories. Diagnostic reports and temporary operational identifiers are processed locally on the device or managed automatically through official platform channels (such as Apple's developer diagnostics tool framework) and are retained only as long as necessary for stability analysis.

3. Third-Party Services

The application may display standard mobile advertisements. These third-party advertising networks process device identifiers independently according to their respective privacy frameworks to maintain ad frequencies and system operations.

4. Children's Privacy

Stage Rummy: Duck Card Game does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. The game is designed to be a safe, casual card puzzle experience for general audiences.
